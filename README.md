Two lines explaining rain to a 10-year-old: a vivid bathtub analogy, then an honest account of what it maps and what it leaves out.

## Entry

> Rain is the sky tipping a giant bathtub over the city, one drop at a time, because the clouds got too heavy to hold the water any longer.
>
> The bathtub shows how rain falls when something up high gets too full to hold its water, but it leaves out that clouds are not a tub at all, just tiny drops of water and dust floating until they grow too heavy to stay up.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the two lines to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
